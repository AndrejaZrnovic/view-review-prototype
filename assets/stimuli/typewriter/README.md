# Typewriter angle study

## Source and rights

`original-front.jpg` is a 1,280-pixel derivative of the real [Woodstock typewriter, 1940s photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Woodstock_typewriter,_1940s.jpg) by Cbaile19, published under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The photo shows only the front and top of one worn black machine. The twelve `angle-*.png` files are AI-generated research stimuli, not documentary photographs of its unseen sides or back.

`contact-sheet.jpg` previews all twelve generated angles. Use the individual PNGs to inspect small labels and mechanisms.

## Interpretation

These were generated independently from the same single source photo. No discrepancy was intentionally planted or corrected. Inspect key order and legends, left-side controls, carriage lever, ruler markings, platen, and mechanical continuity across views. For example, View K puts `BACK SPACE` on the upper-left control, where the source photo shows `TAB STOP SET`; other labels and ruler numbers also vary. The rear panels and side hardware are inferences and cannot be called true or false from this source alone.

The app records each participant's flags, notes, confidence, and decisions. Its planted-error scores are unavailable for this exploratory set. To score accuracy on the side and rear views, obtain additional real photographs of this same machine and adjudicate each observation against them before collecting participants.
